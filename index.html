M.Sc. in Computer Engineering, Chosun University, South Korea

I work on computer vision and deep learning, with a focus on fine-grained visual classification, localized feature learning, and lightweight models for spoof fingerprint detection. I completed my master's as a Research Assistant in the Computer Vision Lab at Chosun University under Professor Ho Yub Jung. Earlier, I worked on bioinformatics and machine learning for healthcare. I am interested in pursuing a PhD in explainable AI and cybersecurity.
