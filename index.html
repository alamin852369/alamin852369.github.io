Computer vision researcher working on fine-grained visual classification, explainable AI and biometric security.

I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Chosun University, South Korea, where I worked as a Research Assistant in the Computer Vision Lab under Professor Ho Yub Jung on a fully funded scholarship. Before that, I worked on bioinformatics and machine learning for healthcare. I am now looking to pursue a PhD in explainable AI and cybersecurity.

Research Interests

The areas I work in and want to keep exploring.

Computer Vision

Teaching machines to understand images, the core of most of my research.

Fine-Grained Visual Classification

Telling apart very similar categories, such as bird species or car models, using localized and patch-wise features.

Explainable AI

Making deep learning models transparent, so we can see why a model makes a decision. A main focus for my PhD.

Biometric Security

Protecting fingerprint systems from fake inputs with lightweight spoof detection networks.

Deep Learning

Designing efficient neural network building blocks, such as the Meta Convolution Layer (MCL).

Image Processing

Preparing and enhancing image data so models can learn the right visual details.

Natural Language Processing

Text classification and sentiment analysis, including spam detection and news analysis.

Bioinformatics

Using statistical and machine learning methods to find disease biomarkers from gene data.
